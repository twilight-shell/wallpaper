# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Until 1.0, a minor version
may change how things work.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-24

The first release.

- A picture on the background layer of every screen, revealed through a growing circle when it changes.
- Live stars: an LED wall of drifting, twinkling stars in the colors of the palette.
- `next`, `prev`, `set`, `palette` and `live` over IPC; the picture and the palette are kept in `theme.toml`.
